# Privacy Policy - Quick TAB Launcher by Bonn

**Last updated:** 2026-05-22

## What data this extension accesses

Quick TAB Launcher reads the following browser data for search:

- **Open tabs**: title, URL, and favicon of your open tabs, to let you find and switch between them.
- **Bookmarks**: title and URL of your bookmarks, to include them in search results.
- **Browsing history**: title, URL, visit count, and last visit time from the past 30 days (configurable), to include recent pages in search results.
- **Recently closed tabs**: session data for recently closed tabs, to let you restore them.
- **Page content** (optional, off by default): text content of open tabs, only when you enable full-text search in settings.

## What data this extension stores

Configuration preferences (result limits, search options, language) are saved in `browser.storage.local`. No personal data is stored.

## What data this extension sends

None. The extension makes no network requests. All data stays in your browser. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere - no analytics, no telemetry.

## Permissions explained

- **tabs**: read tab titles and URLs for search.
- **bookmarks**: search your bookmarks.
- **history**: search your browsing history.
- **sessions**: access recently closed tabs for restore.
- **storage**: save your configuration preferences locally.
- **activeTab**: interact with the currently active tab when you invoke the launcher.
- **all_urls (host permission)**: inject the launcher overlay (a content script) into web pages so it can appear on any site you visit.
